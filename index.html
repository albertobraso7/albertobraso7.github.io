02 / ENGINEERING APPROACH

Engineering approach.

Each study connects a design question with suitable models, checked results and engineering recommendations.

01

Define the study

Define the operating conditions, required outputs and design question.

02

Set up the model

Select the mesh, boundary conditions and physical models for the flow and thermal behaviour.

03

Check the results

Check convergence, conservation and sensitivity. Compare with measurements or published benchmarks when available.

04

Report the findings

Explain the flow behaviour, compare designs and report the model’s limitations.
