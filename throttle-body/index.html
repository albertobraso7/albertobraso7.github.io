PROFESSIONAL PROJECT / VIRTUAL CHARACTERIZATION

Use a measurable valve to establish the method.

The testing limit

The available bench could characterize a smaller throttle, but could not supply the flow needed for larger valves. Missing flow curves constrained development and calibration.

My contribution

I checked CFD against measurements on the smaller valve, then applied the method across openings and pressure conditions for the larger units, including WOT and partial-opening work.

What it enabled

The resulting mass-flow curves supplied engineering data that the existing bench could not provide. The workflow covered naturally aspirated and turbocharged applications.

The selected image shows a generic geometry and flow field. Validation and delivered industrial curves belong to the professional project; they are not measurements of this public example.

01 / DEFINE A USEFUL COMPARISON

Opening angle and pressure must be studied together.

A valve does not have one mass-flow value. Its opening and upstream/downstream conditions define the operating point.

Build the characterization matrix

The professional campaign combined pressure conditions with valve openings, checked the model where test data existed, and delivered curves for subsequent engineering use. Consistent pressure references and reporting locations made those comparisons meaningful.

Explain the mechanism

The public Mach and pressure panels show why that matrix needs compressible CFD: the restriction accelerates the gas, changes its density and creates downstream expansion and compression structures.

The private Python drivers automate one configurable mesh/setup workflow; they do not generate an automatic multi-angle geometry sweep.

02 / FLOW INTERPRETATION

Acceleration and pressure variation around the plate

The two panels describe the same type of engineering question from different quantities: where the gas accelerates and how static pressure varies through the restriction.

Mach number

The upper panel shows acceleration around the plate and a downstream jet with local supersonic regions. The compression and expansion pattern is a reason to resolve density and energy rather than assume constant-density flow.

Normalized static pressure

The lower panel relates local static pressure to inlet and outlet reference values. These visualization references differ from the solver’s inlet total-pressure condition. Negative normalized values indicate pressure below the chosen outlet reference; they do not imply negative absolute pressure.

Normalization makes the pattern readable without disclosing an industrial pressure condition. It does not establish that different operating points have the same flow behavior.

For characterization, combine the field view with mass flow, pressure definitions, valve opening and convergence evidence for each operating point. This image alone does not demonstrate validation or an optimized component.

03 / REUSABLE WORKFLOW

Separate meshing from solver setup

Two Python drivers keep model configuration readable and make repeated setup less dependent on manual Fluent sessions.

Mesh generation

Watertight Geometry with specified surface sizes, smooth-transition boundary layers and poly-hexcore volume meshing. The WFT reference records the selected settings.

Compressible model

Steady density-based implicit solver, energy, ideal-gas air, Sutherland viscosity and k–ω SST. Named expressions define pressure conditions; measurement planes support the reports.

Setup and run modes

The setup driver creates surfaces and base reports before compound expressions. Separate setup, run and all modes preserve the documented initialization and iteration procedure.

Verification and reproduction limits

Earlier development notes record matching reference/rebuilt mesh counts and individual solver API checks. Equal counts do not establish mesh independence, and a complete converged 2,500-iteration run was not demonstrated. No solver was run for this publication.

CAD and meshes are excluded and must be supplied locally. The documentation identifies checks still needed for operating pressure, measurement-plane placement and report validity. The cd_a expression is an equivalent area in m², not a dimensionless discharge coefficient or a general compressible-flow correction.
