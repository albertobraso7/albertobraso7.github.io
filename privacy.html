Privacy and analytics

This portfolio is maintained by Alberto Brambila. Optional Google Analytics helps me understand which engineering projects visitors explore and whether they choose to view my CV or contact links.

Your choice

Google Analytics loads only after you accept, and only on albertobraso7.github.io. You can reject it and use the whole portfolio. Use Analytics preferences at the bottom of any page to change your choice. Rejecting after acceptance stops collection, removes this site's accessible analytics cookies and reloads the page. Your preference is stored locally for up to 180 days.

What is measured

With consent, Google receives page views, selected link and download clicks, estimated section visibility, video interactions, performance measurements and limited error categories. Google also processes online identifiers and technical request information, including IP address, to produce approximate location and device statistics. Analytics cookies can distinguish browsers across visits; these reports do not identify a particular recruiter or employer.

Custom events omit email addresses, free text, form contents, full query strings, detailed error messages and stack traces. Public page paths are allowlisted; external referrer paths are removed. Only predefined campaign labels are retained. There are no advertising features, session recordings or fingerprinting added by this site. A click on a CV is download intent, and a click on email is contact intent—not proof of a completed download or sent message.

Service and retention

Google Analytics is provided by Google. Processing may take place outside your country. See Google's privacy policy and Google Analytics data safeguards. First-party analytics cookies are configured for up to 180 days; event-data retention is separately controlled in the Analytics property. This page does not promise that browser storage controls erase records previously received by Google.

GitHub Pages delivers this website independently of optional analytics. See GitHub's privacy statement for hosting-related processing. External sites have their own policies.
